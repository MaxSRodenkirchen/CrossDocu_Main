
# Welcome Fellow Learner ^-.-^

**Exploring Creative Technologies** is a **Public Learning Experience** in the form of a **Digital Garden**. You wonder what the heck all these terms mean? Start here:

- [[In One Sentence]]
- [[Why This Garden Began]]
- [[About Learning in Public]]
- [[About Digital Gardens and Streams]]

### How to Navigate

You can easily navigate through this site using only the internal links. [[This Gardens System]]
You can also collapse the sidebar using the button in the bottom-left corner. If you prefer a more guided overview, feel free to browse the lists in the sidebar or use the search function.

### About the Learner

This public exploration is part of Max S. Rodenkirchen’s Master’s in Design, focusing on Creative Technologies. Its purpose is to train communication skills, deepen expertise, and support exchange within the field.

- Design Class: [[Der Baum hat Zeit]]
- [Other projects by Max S. Rodenkirchen](https://linktree.max-rodenkirchen.de/)
