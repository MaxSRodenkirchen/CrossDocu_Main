# Learning in Public

**Share your process and support exchange!**

Learning in public is a nice concept for anyone interested in exchange. The core idea is that we share our learning process. This could be on a small community scale but also to a a larger audience via the internet. There are two benefits from sharing your learnings. The first thing is that you train yourself in documenting and explaining your expertise to others. Depending on how complex your work is this can be a quite challenging but also very rewarding task. The other benefit is that because you share, others can comment on you. They might say: "Oh, that's cool. Have you also heard about this...?" or "You have a point there, but in my opinion it's more like this...". What happens is that you are now in exchange and start learning from one another. 

This is exactly what this Digital Garden is about!
