# About VCV Rack

![VCV Rack](https://vcvrack.com/manual/images/templatepatch.png)
*Image: [vcvrack.com](https://vcvrack.com/manual/images/templatepatch.png)*

VCV Rack is a modular software synthesizer in the Eurorack format. The Eurorack is a standard for modular hardware synthesizers developed by Dieter Doepfer. VCV Rack is open source and has a large community of people developing synth modules. 

If you want to learn audio synthesis I would recommend looking into [Red Means Recording on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BTcP3DofZLY&t=10s). He has some videos where he explains the fundamentals in VCV Rack.

Since VCV Rack is free it's way better suited for starting and testing out audio synthesis. But if you like this way of making music definitely try out a modular hardware synth somewhere. With to hands you can twist more knobs and grab cables. In general it feels more like playing an instrument. 

A benefit of VCV Rack being a open source software is that we can quite easily connect it to other software. [[TouchDesigner to VCV Rack]]
