# Der Baum hat Zeit

or *"The Tree Has Time"* is the title of the design class in summer **2026 by Prof. Eva Vitting at FH Aachen**. The course is designed to provide a common direction for the students’ self-selected projects. In terms of media, however, the projects take very different approaches. Many students are working in the areas of interactive, game-based media or infographic visualizations. This Digital Garden was created as part of this course. [[Why This Garden Began]]
